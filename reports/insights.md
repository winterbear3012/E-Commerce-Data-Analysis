# Olist E-Commerce Data Analysis — Insights

## Overview

- Total orders: 99,441
- Total payment value: 16,008,872.16
- Average order value: 160.99
- Average review score: 4.09
- Average delivery time: 12.56 days
- Average freight value per item: 19.99
- Total freight value: 2,251,909.54

## Key Insights

### Revenue

- Health & Beauty generated the highest product-category revenue at 1,258,681.34.
- Watches & Gifts generated 1,205,005.68.
- Bed & Bath Table generated 1,036,988.68.
- São Paulo generated the highest customer-state revenue at 5,998,226.96.
- São Paulo was also the highest-revenue customer city at 2,203,373.09.

### Orders

- Delivered orders accounted for 96,478 of the 99,441 orders.
- São Paulo had the highest number of orders with 41,746.

### Payments

- Credit cards were the most frequently used payment method, with 76,795 payment records.
- Credit card payments also represented the largest payment value at 12,542,084.19.

### Customer Reviews

- The average review score was 4.09 out of 5.
- 5-star reviews were the most common, with 56,910 reviews.
- 1-star reviews accounted for 11,282 reviews.

### Delivery and Freight

- Average delivery time was 12.56 days from purchase to customer delivery.
- Average freight value per order item was 19.99.
- Total freight value was 2,251,909.54.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows strong order activity concentrated in São Paulo and high revenue contribution from categories such as Health & Beauty and Watches & Gifts. Credit cards were the dominant payment method, while customer reviews were generally positive with an average score of 4.09. Delivery and freight costs are also important areas for evaluating e-commerce operational performance.
